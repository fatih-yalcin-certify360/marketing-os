# Risk register

the single developer/product owner; items needing another party say so.

**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-09. Review at the end of each phase.

Likelihood/impact are Low / Medium / High. "Verified" means a test or a
documented check demonstrates the control — not that someone believes it works.

## Accepted risks

**R-11**, **R-14** and **R-17** are accepted as stated. Everything else is open and must
be revisited at the end of its phase.

## Explicitly not claimed

- Not "zero vulnerabilities".
- Not certified against ISO 9001 or ISO 27001, and no control numbers are cited.
- No capacity figure beyond what was actually measured, under the conditions
  recorded in `docs/architecture/load-assumptions.md`. In particular: nothing is
  claimed about model latency, provider throughput, a real network, more than
  one replica, sustained load beyond 30 seconds, or image-heavy workloads.
- Not that the OpenAI integration is proven under adverse conditions. One
  successful end-to-end run happened on 2026-09-10; real rate limits, outages
  and image generation remain unexercised (**R-16**).
